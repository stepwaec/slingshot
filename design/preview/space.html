Space environment, v1.4 assets built to the v1.5 bar — gas clouds, debris fields, moons, ringed planets

Inline verbatim copies of design/assets/space/*.svg, bodies/moon-*.svg and bodies/ring-*.svg. Soft gas with crisp rims and dust lanes, faceted rocks and metal lit from the upper-right, moons and ring particles on inclined orbits that pass behind and in front of their planet. Everything is slow: cross-fades of 40 s, tumbles of 20–200 s, orbits of 30–130 s. Gameplay hooks named in the captions are optional and not in scope. Static renders show t = 0.

Gas cloudsall at 1×, over a sample starfield (backdrop only, not part of the assets)

Debris fieldsasteroid field at 1×, wreck debris at 1.5× (600 × 390 px)

Moonsicy, volcanic and captured at 4×, planet with moons at 2× (720 × 520 px)

Ringed planetsSaturn-like at 1.6× (640 × 384 px), debris and shepherd at 2× (640 × 400 px)

Compositionemission nebula + asteroid field + Saturn-like ringed planet together at 1× (1280 × 560 px)

Each asset is ONE top-level group (#nebulaEmission, #nebulaReflection, #nebulaDark, #asteroidField, #wreckDebris, #moonIcy … #ringShepherd) with geometry relative to (0,0); lighting never rotates. The planets and stars are in bodies.html. Open this file from file://, no scripts needed — see style.md §9, canvas-notes.md §2b/§4c–§4e and assets.md.
