Body library, v1.5 — 12 planets, 3 ringed planets, 4 moons, 6 stars & compact objects, animated

Inline verbatim copies of design/assets/bodies/*.svg, planet-x.svg and star.svg, built to the two approved references (planet F, sun J): vivid zoned surfaces with crisp edges, a warm terminator band, lit-side haze and rim, fiery plasma stars with ragged flames and rims. SMIL scrolls the texture strips, drifts the gas bands, orbits the moons and the double planet, turns the ring particles, breathes the hazes and fires the prominences and flares; the lighting stays fixed toward the upper-right (planet X: lower-left, toward the scene star). Static renders show t = 0.

Relative sizes — all 25 at 1×1 px = 1 game unit at zoom 1, centres on one line; the strip scrolls sideways when the window is narrower than 3740 px. The stars are spaced so their hazes only meet in their faint outer tails, so each reads on its own. Stars and compact objects list their crash radius (= body / event-horizon radius); rings, orbits, hazes and the capture ring reach beyond the body radius.

Every body is ONE top-level group (#rocky … #blackHole) with geometry relative to (0,0); texture strips scroll west → east over their period, lighting never rotates.
