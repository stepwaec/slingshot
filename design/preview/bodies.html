Stars & compact objectssun / red dwarf / blue giant at 1.5×, white dwarf at 2×, neutron star at 3×, black hole at 2×

Relative sizes — all 18 at 1×1 px = 1 game unit at zoom 1, on one baseline; the strip scrolls sideways when the window is narrower than 2890 px. The stars are spaced so their glows only meet in their faint outer tails (≈ 0.85 of each glow radius), so each reads on its own. Stars and compact objects list their crash radius (= body / event-horizon radius); the sun's corona and the ring reach beyond their tiles.

Every body is ONE top-level group (#rocky … #blackHole) with geometry relative to (0,0); texture strips scroll west → east over their period, lighting never rotates. Open this file from file://, no scripts needed — see style.md §8, canvas-notes.md §3b/§4b and assets.md.
